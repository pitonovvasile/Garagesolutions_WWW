About transmission systems

The transmission system transfers engine torque to the driven wheels and defines how power is converted into acceleration and cruising efficiency. Core components include clutch and flywheel assemblies, manual/automatic gearboxes, differential units, axle interfaces, and transmission-specific oils. FWD layouts package most components at the front axle, RWD layouts split gearbox and rear diff for balanced load paths, while AWD systems add transfer/distribution complexity across both axles. Correct oil intervals, mount/bushing health, and clutch/diff servicing are essential for shifting precision, drivetrain durability, and thermal stability under road or motorsport use.
